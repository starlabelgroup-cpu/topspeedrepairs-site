How Top Speed Appliance LLC handles the information you share when you book or ask a question.

Who we are

Top Speed Appliance LLC is an appliance repair and installation company serving 65 cities across Broward, Palm Beach and Miami-Dade counties, Florida. We are a service-area business — repairs happen at your home or business. This site is topspeedrepairs.com; bookings run on the Top Speed Service OS platform.

What we collect

When you book or contact us we collect the details the job needs: your name, mobile phone, email (optional), the service address, your city and ZIP, the appliance and what it is doing, and your preferred day and window. If you chat with Speedy, the conversation is kept with your request so a Service Setter can confirm your visit.

How we use it

  • To schedule, confirm and run your visit — including confirmation calls and texts, and the live Track My Tech link when your technician is on the way.
  • To quote, invoice and support the work, and to run the Refer & Earn program if you take part.
  • To keep the platform secure and understand which pages and cities need attention, in aggregate.

Sharing

Your details go to the Service Setters dispatch team and the technician assigned to your job, and to the messaging and payment providers that carry our confirmations and invoices. We do not sell your personal information. We share information only when the law requires it.

Your choices

Reply STOP to any text to stop messages, and call us at (844) 460-7038 to update or delete your details. Deleting booking details can limit our ability to service warranty work on a past job.

Contact

Questions about privacy: call (844) 460-7038, Mon–Fri 8am–7pm.
