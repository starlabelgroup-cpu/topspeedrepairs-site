Service

Top Speed Appliance LLC provides appliance repair and installation at your home or business across 65 cities in Broward, Palm Beach and Miami-Dade counties. Business hours are Monday–Friday, 8am–7pm. Every booking is confirmed by a human Service Setter — an online request is not a confirmed appointment until we confirm it.

Diagnostic

Residential service starts with a $99 diagnostic, collected at booking to reserve your window. The diagnostic is credited in full toward an approved repair performed by Top Speed. If you decline the repair, the diagnostic remains due for the visit and the findings.

Flat labor and written quotes

Repair labor is $210 flat and installation labor is $150 flat. Parts and materials are priced in a written quote on site. No work is done without your approved written quote, and there is no hourly billing.

Commercial work

Commercial service is priced by custom quote only, through Appliance Pro.

Warranty

Repairs carry a 90-day parts and labor warranty on the work performed. The warranty covers the repaired fault; unrelated faults and pre-existing conditions are quoted as new work.

Cancellations

Call (844) 460-7038 to move or cancel a visit. Windows are held on a first-confirmed basis; weather and flooding can move windows for safety, and we will always call you first.
